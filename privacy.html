Privacy Policy

2048 Multiplayer · Last updated: November 13, 2026

2048 Multiplayer is operated by Scalera Technologies ("we", "us", or "our"). This policy explains what information the game collects, why we collect it, and the choices you have.

Contact: scalera.support@gmail.com
App package: com.scalera.game2048

1. Scope

This policy applies to the 2048 Multiplayer mobile game and its online multiplayer features. It does not apply to third-party websites or services that we do not control.

2. Information we collect

  • Generated display name. We assign each install a display name from a curated list of words. You cannot enter a free-text name. Your display name is visible to opponents during online play.
  • Anonymous account identifiers. We use Firebase Anonymous Authentication, which creates a random user ID and a local client ID. We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, or password.
  • Gameplay data. Room codes, board states, scores, move counts, queue and match records, connection status, and match results. This data is used to run multiplayer matches and reconnect you to an active match.
  • Device and technical data. Device and app information, IP address, approximate region, and diagnostic logs processed by our service providers when the app connects to their services.
  • Advertising data. When ads are shown, Google AdMob may process your advertising identifier, IP address, device information, ad requests, and ad interactions. Consent choices are handled through Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP).
  • Purchase data. If you buy Remove Ads, Google Play and RevenueCat process the product, transaction status, and entitlement. We do not receive or store your payment card details.
  • Local storage on your device. Settings, statistics, your generated name, undo count, and entitlement cache are stored locally so the game can work offline. Clearing app data or uninstalling the app removes this local data.

3. How we use information

  • To create and run online rooms, matchmaking, and live matches.
  • To restore your session after a disconnect or app restart.
  • To prevent abuse, debug crashes, and keep the game secure.
  • To show ads, including rewarded ads that grant in-game undos.
  • To process and restore purchases.
  • To understand aggregate usage and improve the game, if analytics are enabled.

4. Legal bases

Where data protection laws such as the GDPR or UK GDPR apply, we rely on: performance of a contract (running the game and multiplayer features), legitimate interests (security, debugging, and improving the game), and consent (personalized advertising where required). You can change ad consent choices through the in-app Privacy Options control.

5. Sharing and service providers

We do not sell personal information. We share data only with providers that help us run the game:

  • Google Firebase — anonymous authentication and Realtime Database.
  • Google AdMob — advertising and consent management.
  • Google Play — app distribution and purchase processing.
  • RevenueCat — purchase and entitlement management, when purchases are enabled.

These providers process data under their own privacy policies. We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect the safety and rights of users.

6. Advertising and consent

The game may show rewarded ads and interstitials. Where required, we ask for consent before serving personalized ads. You can revisit your choices from the game's Privacy Options control. Even if you decline personalized ads, non-personalized ads may still be shown. You can also reset or limit your advertising ID in your device settings.

Rewarded ads are optional and grant in-game undos only after the ad provider confirms a completed view. We do not reward clicks.

7. Data retention

Active rooms, queue tickets, and match records are kept only as long as needed to run and secure the game and are cleaned up periodically. Local data remains on your device until you clear app data or uninstall the app. Advertising and purchase records are retained by Google and RevenueCat according to their policies.

8. Security

Data sent to Firebase, AdMob, Google Play, and RevenueCat is transmitted over encrypted connections. We use database rules to limit access to rooms and user data. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Children

The game is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will take appropriate action.

10. Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict processing of your information, or to object to certain processing. Because authentication is anonymous, we may not be able to identify your records from your email address alone; we may ask for your anonymous user ID or room codes.

  • You can clear local data by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it.
  • You can change ad consent choices from Privacy Options.
  • You can contact us at scalera.support@gmail.com to request deletion or ask a privacy question.
  • You can lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

11. International transfers

Our service providers may process information in countries other than your own, including the United States. They use safeguards required by applicable law for international transfers.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the game changes. We will post the updated version at this URL and revise the "Last updated" date. Material changes will be communicated in the app or store listing where appropriate.

13. Contact

Questions or requests: scalera.support@gmail.com
